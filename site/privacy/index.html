Privacy

Everything stays on your device

LucidDream has no accounts, no server of its own, and no analytics. There is nowhere for your data to go, because there is nothing on the other end.

What the app stores, and where

Your signals, your scripts, your settings and your run logs live in the app's own storage on the device — in app storage on Android, and in the browser's own storage in the web version. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

A run log leaves the device only when you export it yourself, through the share sheet, and then it goes wherever you send it.

What ever leaves the device

  • Files you ask for. When you add a script or a signal from a URL, the app fetches that URL. That is a request to whoever hosts it — this site, if the file came from here.
  • Nothing else. No account, no sync, no crash reporting, no usage statistics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs of any kind.

The microphone

The optional gentle interrupt uses the microphone to answer one question: how loud is the room right now. It reads a level in decibels and nothing else. There is no speech recognition — the app cannot tell a spoken word from a snore, and does not try.

While that setting is on, the system audio recorder writes to a temporary file in the app's own private storage as a side effect of measuring. LucidDream never reads that file, never puts it in your log, and never sends it anywhere. Leave the setting off and the app never asks for the microphone at all.

This website

These pages set no cookies, load no fonts or scripts from other companies, and carry no analytics. The site is served by Cloudflare, which — like any web host — sees ordinary request information such as IP addresses in its own logs.

Two pages make a request while you read them: the download button asks GitHub's public API for the current release, and the script library asks this site for its own catalogue. Neither sends anything about you.

Children, and health data

LucidDream is not directed at children, collects nothing from anyone, and is not a medical device. It is an experimental self-study tool. Nothing it records is health information held by us, because we hold nothing.
